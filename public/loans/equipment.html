What Equipment Can You Finance?

Almost any revenue-generating equipment qualifies. We work with vendors directly or reimburse you after purchase.

Construction & Heavy Machinery

Excavators, bulldozers, skid steers, cranes, dump trucks, and compactors. Both new and used equipment up to 10 years old typically qualifies. Title transfers to you at the end of the term.

Medical & Dental Equipment

X-ray machines, diagnostic imaging, dental chairs, surgical lasers, and practice management software. We understand medical practice cash flow and structure payments accordingly.

Restaurant & Hospitality

Commercial ovens, walk-in coolers, POS systems, espresso machines, and food trucks. Lease-to-own options available for businesses that want to preserve working capital.

Manufacturing & CNC

CNC machines, 3D printers, injection molders, packaging lines, and quality control equipment. We work with specialized manufacturing lenders who understand asset resale value.

Transportation & Fleet

Semi-trucks, box trucks, delivery vans, and trailers. We can structure fleet deals and work with both owner-operators and logistics companies.

Technology & Office

Servers, networking equipment, phone systems, and software licenses. Technology financing often includes installation and training costs in the loan amount.
