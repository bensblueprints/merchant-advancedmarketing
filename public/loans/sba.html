SBA Programs We Offer

We work with both major SBA programs to find the right fit for your business goals.

SBA 7(a) Loan

The most flexible SBA program. Use it for working capital, equipment, inventory, business acquisition, or debt refinancing. Loans up to $5M. Terms up to 10 years for equipment and 25 years for real estate.

SBA 504 Loan

Designed specifically for major fixed assets like real estate and heavy equipment. Typically requires 10% down payment. Fixed-rate financing with terms up to 25 years. Great for owner-occupied commercial property.
